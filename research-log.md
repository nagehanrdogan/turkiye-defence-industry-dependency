# Research log

Dated, append-only notes on the dependency-mapping work. Unlike [`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md) (a periodically-refreshed summary of the current state), entries here are not rewritten — they're a running record of what was checked, when, and what came of it.

---

### 2026-07-31

- **Closed a coverage gap in the SIPRI pull.** The initial SIPRI Trade Register download only covered delivery years 1995–2025. Since IISS/CFPPR's procurement tables start in 1982, this meant any locally-produced design whose deliveries were entirely pre-1995 was silently absent — not flagged as unverified, just missing. Re-pulled 1950–1994 with the column set that includes SIPRI's "Local production" field (the quick Trade Register export omits it; the full transfer-data export with "deliveries broken down by year" includes it) and found 12 designs with no prior record: AGM-12B Bullpup, Bell-205A-1, Berk-class, COBRA MI, EDIC, Fieldguard, MEKO-200T, PC-1638, Rapier-1, SAR-33, SF-260, Type-209/1200. Added as unverified pending external corroboration.
- **Verified 8 previously-unconfirmed designs** against independent sources (manufacturer/press reporting): ATR-72MP, Göktürk-1, K-10, CN-235 ASW, GDF 35mm, ESSM, AIFV, and M-52T. One design (a "Comandante"-class patrol ship entry) was actively checked and could not be confirmed — Fincantieri's Comandante-class vessels appear to be Italian-Navy-only, with no located link to Turkey; left unverified rather than guessed at.
- **Reconstructed platform-evolution cases** from IISS's own per-program acquisition-type coding (cross-referencing the same platform family across different years): S-70A Black Hawk (1992, off-the-shelf) → T-70 Black Hawk (2014, licensed production, same supplier); F-4E Phantom (1995, foreign-assisted upgrade) → indigenous "Şimşek" upgrade (2006, no foreign supplier recorded); Barbaros-class frigate (1990, German-licensed) → 2018 modernization (fully domestic); and a reverse case — Anka UAV (2004, no foreign supplier recorded) → Anka-3 (2022, Ukrainian engine dependency appears) — a reminder that dependency doesn't move in only one direction over a platform's life.
- **Re-ran the temporal co-occurrence test** (see [`criticality_matrix/README.md`](criticality_matrix/README.md)) end-to-end on the current data: 36/36 locally-produced designs (100%) return at least one candidate subsystem match within a ±2-year window, confirming this is not a usable filter on its own. Documented as a negative result rather than discarded quietly.

### 2026-08-03

- **Cleared almost all of the single-source (IISS/CFPPR-only) backlog.** Of the 86 designs flagged 2026-07-31 as resting on IISS/CFPPR alone, all but 2 (Bell 412EP, MIM-23 HAWK) are now corroborated by an independent second source, prioritizing US-sourced platforms first (DSCA notifications) per plan. The 2 remaining were actively searched and could not be confirmed with confidence — left unresolved rather than guessed at.
- **Caught and corrected a sourcing error.** Initially recorded MiKES as an ASELSAN subsidiary based on company-aggregator sites (PitchBook, EMIS, Datanyze). Checked against ASELSAN's own "İştirakler" (subsidiaries) page and its KAP (Turkish Public Disclosure Platform) filing — MiKES is absent from both official primary sources. Reclassified as "araştırıldı ama doğrulanamadı" rather than trusting the aggregator claim. Company-aggregator sites are not a substitute for an official primary source, even when they agree with each other.
- **Fixed a labeling ambiguity affecting 13 rows.** These carried the status text "DOĞRULANMIŞ (IISS/CFPPR raporu)," which reads as fully corroborated but in fact rested on IISS/CFPPR alone for the specific foreign-supplier claim — SIPRI's own "local production" flag only confirms that local production happened, not who the supplier was. A first pass fixed this by relabeling ("2 kurumsal kaynak"), which was correctly rejected as insufficient since it added no actual independent source for the supplier claim itself. Resolved properly: 8 of the 13 were already cross-referenced to independently-verified duplicate rows (the same program recorded twice under different names across separate SIPRI/IISS pulls — e.g. Yavuz=MEKO-200T, Barbaros=MEKO-200T2, Kılıç=Kılıç(56), M-60T Sabra-2=M60T); the remaining 5 (AS-532 Cougar, CN-235MP, A-400M, Rapier-2, B-737 AEW/Peace Eagle) were independently re-verified against primary/manufacturer sources (Airbus, Boeing, Army/Airforce/Naval Technology, Aviation Week, GlobalSecurity.org).
- **Workbook restructuring.** Merged the two IISS procurement-table sheets (1982–2003, 2004–2022) into one sheet with an added period column; consolidated the two SIPRI Trade Register CSV pulls (1950–1994 and 1995–2025) into a single in-workbook sheet with added import/export direction tagging.
- **New duplicate-platform cross-references found** while resolving the above, beyond those already logged: Type-209/1400=Preveze, Kılıç=Kılıç(56), Frankenthal=Aydın, M-60T Sabra-2=M60T, KT-1≈KT-1T, F-16C=Peace Onyx I, Stinger=FIM-92 Stinger — each documented in place rather than merged or silently duplicated.
- **Net effect on coverage:** of 141 designs, 131 (93%) are now corroborated by two or more independent sources, 2 (1%) remain single-source and unresolved despite active search, and 8 (6%) are partially confirmed or still pending. See updated numbers in [`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md).
